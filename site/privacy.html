We are the controller of the personal data described here. This policy covers the FounderBrain connector for Claude, including the report it shows in a chat. Pages on thinkreasonlearn.com, such as this one, are served by our web host, which receives your IP address and browser type when you visit. The FounderBrain pages load their fonts from the same host; other pages on the site may load fonts from Google Fonts.

Privacy questions and requests go to support@thinkreasonlearn.com. Security reports go to security@thinkreasonlearn.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer.

What FounderBrain does

FounderBrain analyses one speaker in a meeting transcript. It checks each of their answers against 100 fixed questions about how people speak, such as whether an answer gives a number or tells a story, and compares the result with 773 public interviews of founders and operators. It returns a short report about that one conversation, which Claude uses to coach you. The transcript is scored on our server and is not stored.

This is a form of profiling, because it describes how a person spoke. The report describes one conversation, not a person's ability, character or suitability for anything. FounderBrain makes no decisions about anyone, and its report should never be the only basis for a decision about a person, such as whether to hire them or invest in them.

What we receive

When Claude runs FounderBrain, our server receives what the tool call carries.

  • The transcript text, which Claude sends from your chat so that our server can score it. We use it only for that analysis and do not store it.
  • The speaker label you chose and whether they were mainly answering or asking questions.
  • A display name, if the transcript uses a generic label such as "Me" or "Speaker 2" and you gave Claude a name. It appears in the report.
  • Technical data that any web request carries, such as an IP address, the time, the size of the request and the client software. In Claude on the web and in the desktop app, tool calls come from Anthropic's servers, so we do not see your IP address. Tools that connect directly, such as Claude Code, send requests from your own device, so we see your address.
  • When Claude shows the report in a chat, your browser loads the report's script and fonts from our server. That request carries your IP address, your browser type and the address of the page showing the report.

We do not ask for your name, email address or an account, and FounderBrain has no sign in. Using FounderBrain is optional. Without a transcript we cannot produce a report.

Where it is processed

Our server runs in Google Cloud's us-central1 region in the United States, and our email is handled by Google Workspace. We are based in the United States and process your data there, so if you are in the UK or the European Economic Area your data is transferred to the United States. Our processor Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, and Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum also includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum. You can ask us for details of these safeguards.

People in your transcript

A transcript usually contains other people's words. Only the speaker you choose is scored. The questions put to them are used alongside their answers as context, and the labels of the other speakers are shown to Claude so that you can pick one. Nothing about anyone in the transcript is kept once the analysis ends.

Use FounderBrain to analyse yourself, or someone who knows about it and agrees. Please send only transcripts you have the right to share.

If you were in a meeting that someone analysed with FounderBrain, the rights below apply to you too. Because we keep no copy of the transcript and have no way to contact you, we cannot tell you when it happens.

Well-known people in reports

Reports compare the speaker with well-known founders, CEOs and operators, and name those people. The comparison comes from public interviews, including Lenny's Podcast, Mixergy, and public podcast, television and stage interviews. For each person we hold style scores and habit rates derived from their public answers, and a short description of what they are known for. Only people whom most readers would recognise are named in reports.

If you are one of these people and want to object or be removed, write to support@thinkreasonlearn.com.

Sensitive information

Transcripts can mention health, politics, religion or other sensitive matters. FounderBrain measures how people speak, not what they speak about. It does not extract or keep what people say about sensitive matters, and one of its measures notes only whether a speaker shares a personal experience or emotion. Please remove anything you do not need analysed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your personal data, to receive it in a portable form, and to object to our processing. Because we keep no transcripts or reports, and our logs and counts are not linked to names, we will usually hold nothing we can find about you. If you give us something that lets us find data about you, such as the IP address and time of a request, we will look and act on your request within one month. If a request is complex, we may take up to two further months, and we will tell you within the first month if we need to.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, write to support@thinkreasonlearn.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or the authority where you live or work in the European Economic Area.

Security

FounderBrain is served only over HTTPS. Plain HTTP requests are redirected and never processed. The server runs under its own account, with access only to its own secrets and its own counts. Its secrets are held in Google's Secret Manager, addresses are only stored as keyed hashes, and transcripts never touch permanent storage. If a data breach affects you, we will tell the relevant authority and, where the law requires it, you.

Everything else

FounderBrain sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. The report in a chat loads only from our own server.

FounderBrain analyses text only. It uses no audio, voice, images or other biometric data. It notes whether answers express emotion in words, such as enthusiasm or a flat tone, but it does not infer anyone's emotional state.

FounderBrain is not meant for anyone under 18, in line with Claude's own age requirement.

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date. For a change that affects how we use your data, we will post a notice on this page at least 14 days before it takes effect.
